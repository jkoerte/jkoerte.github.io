## Introduction
To be honest, I am currently not that knowledgable about software engineering. What knowledge I do have comes from my final year in high school messing around with VSCode, Tailwind CSS and MongoDB, where it was abruptly cut short due to graduation.

However, I am not unwilling to learn. In fact, software engineering is very interesting to me. From the tutorials on CSS, HTML, etc. I have done for my ICS 314 class, the way that just lines of code can turn a blank page into a masterpiece of both design and interactivity is very amazing to me. And now, I can somehwat understand the syntax that appears when you inspect a page on Chrome that has confused me since elementary school.

## What Skills I Hope To Develop
On of the main skills I hope to further develop is my overall programming fundamentals ICS 11 and ICS 211 served as valuable experiences for me, but I fell that ICS 212 is where I really began to understand better. I want to improve on things like knowing where an issue is and how to effectively respond to it, as well as being able to think outside the box for what I might need in order to get my program to do what I want it to. So, I hope that during my time in ICS 214 and the rest of my time at UH Manoa I can really hammer in these fundamentals. Additionally, I hope to grow my knowledge of programming languages. Right now I only have experience with C and Java, so I especially want to learn languages like HTML and CSS which, to my knowledge, are more specififed for web development

## What Experiences I Hope To Have
What I most want to expereience now and in the future is opportunities to write my own programs, but also to learn more about software engineering as a whole. There is always something more to learn, even as I become more knowledgable about the field. I also hope to experience having to program in a team setting since jobs, and maybe even some school projects would liekly require that of you. As such, I believe it would be a very useful experience to have

## Conclusion
Overall, I hope to develop my fundamentals further while also experiencing things that will allow me to grow as both a person and a programmer. I believe all my previous ICS classes, and now ICS 314 will be able to help me achieve that, and in the future, I'm sure there will be many more opportunities to learn and experience great and helpful things.
And the final answer - it's supposed to be difficult, and it's supposed to challenge you, just like everything else that humans do that is difficult: programming, engineering, engaging in relationships, pondering the universe, etc.

Ultimately the question you should really ask yourself if something if particularly difficult is then "is it worth it"? That is something that is context specific and only you can answer yourself.
