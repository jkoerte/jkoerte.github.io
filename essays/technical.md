## The Things That Engineer My Attention
To be honest, I am currently not that knowledgeable about software engineering. What knowledge I do have comes from my final year in high school messing around with development tools like VSCode, Tailwind CSS and MongoDB, where it was abruptly cut short due to graduation.

However, software engineering is very interesting, so I’d like to learn more. I have watched many tutorials on how to style a website with CSS and HTML programming languages, and the way that just lines of code can turn a blank page into a masterpiece of both design and interactivity is absolutely amazing. Now, I have begun to understand some parts out of the thousands of lines of code that appears when using the inspect feature on Chrome, which used to confuse me since elementary school.

## What I Hope To Develop (Besides Software)
One of the main skills I hope to further develop is my overall programming fundamentals. ICS 111 and ICS 211 served as valuable experiences for me, but I feel that ICS 212 is where I really began to explore deeper into how programming languages work, and understand programming better. I want to improve on things like knowing where an issue is and how to effectively respond to it, as well as being able to think outside the box for what I might need in order to get my program to do what I want it to. So, I hope that during my time in ICS 214 and the rest of my time at UH Manoa I can really hammer in these fundamentals. Additionally, I hope to grow my knowledge of programming languages. Right now I only have experience with C and Java, so I especially want to learn languages like HTML and CSS which, to my knowledge, are more specific for web design.

## The More Experiences, The Merrier
What I most want to experience now and in the future is as many opportunities as I can get to write my own programs, but also to learn more about software engineering as a whole. Really, I will take any experience to learn and grow that comes my way. I know just as well as anyone that there is always something more to learn, even as I become more knowledgeable about the field. Finally, I also hope to experience having to program in a team setting since jobs, and maybe even some school projects, will eventually require that of me. As such, I believe it would be a very useful experience to have for my future.

## Conclusion
Overall, I hope to develop my fundamentals further while also experiencing things that will enable me to grow as both a person and a programmer. I believe all my previous ICS classes, and now ICS 314, helped me start to achieve that. In the future, I'm sure there will be many more experiences to be had, and opportunities to learn and grow as a software engineer, team member, and more.
